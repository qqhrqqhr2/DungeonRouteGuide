Dungeon facts / 던전 정보 참고
  Step order, boss / NPC / rare / quest-spot positions, alternate spawn spots,
  quest lists, boss loot (item IDs) and entrance coordinates for WoW Forever
  were referenced from
  WOWF.IO dungeon guides (https://wowf.io/ko/dungeons, accessed 2026-10-06).
  Positions were converted onto the Atlas maps; descriptions were rewritten
  for this addon (quest objective lines are the game's quest text).
  New Forever dungeons (Hall of Thanes, Excavation Site, Ruins of Lordaeron)
  use the hand-drawn sketches above; City of Dalaran is list-only.
