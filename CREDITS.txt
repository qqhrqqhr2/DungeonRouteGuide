Blizzard dungeon map art (default map style)
  The game's own world-map tiles (interface/worldmap/...) are loaded from the
  WoW client at run time by file ID. No Blizzard image is included in this
  addon. File IDs were looked up in the community listfile (wowdev/wow-listfile).
  The new Forever dungeons use the game's minimap tiles of their maps, also
  loaded from the client by file ID (IDs read from each map's WDT file).
  3D boss previews use the client's creature models; display IDs from the
  CMaNGOS classic database (github.com/cmangos/classic-db).
  Which trash mob drops an item (and how often): CMaNGOS classic loot tables
  and spawns; Korean mob names from AzerothCore (github.com/azerothcore).

Maps/Thanes.tga, Maps/Excavation.tga, Maps/RuinsLordaeron.tga, Maps/Dalaran.tga: hand-drawn
sketch maps made for this addon, used when the client has no minimap tiles for them (layout referenced from the WOWF.IO guides; no pixels copied;
Dalaran drawn from the guide text only).

Dungeon facts / 던전 정보 참고
  Step order, boss / NPC / rare / quest-spot positions, alternate spawn spots,
  quest lists, boss loot (item IDs) and entrance coordinates for WoW Forever
  were referenced from
  WOWF.IO dungeon guides (https://wowf.io/ko/dungeons, accessed 2026-10-07).
  Positions are used as they are on the Blizzard maps and were converted onto
  the Atlas maps; descriptions were rewritten
  for this addon (quest objective lines are the game's quest text).
  New Forever dungeons: the wowf positions were fitted onto the minimap tiles;
  City of Dalaran has no positions on WOWF.IO, its boss spots are estimated
  from the guide's area names.
