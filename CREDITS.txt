Dungeon maps / 던전 지도
  The maps are the game's own minimap art (Blizzard Entertainment), taken from
  the WoW Forever Beta 1.60.1.70338 client, with matching Classic Era
  1.15.9.70003 supplements, and bundled in MinimapTiles_v12/. How each page was
  built (tile grids, WMO minimap textures, placement) is in MINIMAP_SOURCES.txt.

Other game data / 기타 자료
  3D boss previews use the client's creature models; display IDs from the
  CMaNGOS classic database (github.com/cmangos/classic-db).
  Which trash mob drops an item (and how often): CMaNGOS classic loot tables
  and spawns; Korean mob names from AzerothCore (github.com/azerothcore).
  Goal and event positions: CMaNGOS classic-db and mangos-classic references.

Dungeon facts / 던전 정보 참고
  Step order, boss / NPC / rare / quest-spot positions, alternate spawn spots,
  quest lists, boss loot (item IDs) and entrance coordinates for WoW Forever
  were referenced from community dungeon guides (accessed 2026-10-07).
  Descriptions were rewritten for this addon (quest objective lines are the
  game's quest text). City of Dalaran boss spots partly come from in-game
  screenshots and partly from the guides' area names.
